# Fidelity gap register

The reference remains original RCT2 gameplay. Implementation candidates may be
developed and compared, but a candidate or modern OpenRCT2-only rule must not
be reported as an original conformance pass. The [acceptance contract](first-playable-acceptance.md)
requires named evidence and Patrick's decision for gameplay-changing tradeoffs.

| Gap | Current evidence / implementation boundary | Required qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Track catalogue, station/portal geometry, legal transitions and support limits | Reconstructed connector/clearance research; production kernel accepts explicit independent profiles. Station groups, side-facing portals and distinct operating/access checks are implemented. Test geometry and centerlines are project-candidate only. | Independently authored selected-family descriptors, matched original placement observations and named legality fixtures. |
| Construction price and demolition refund | Integer accounting/atomic mutations implemented; test prices and refund fraction are candidate fixtures, not original rates. | Original costs/refunds by geometry, operation/age and ownership conditions, with replay observations. |
| Train motion, dispatch, seats and blocks | Research maps dependencies; the operating extension runs an independently authored sampled-motion candidate with station dispatch, train fit and measurements, with individual queue/seat/payment ownership and safe unloading; block operation is outstanding. Disposable ring motion is not used. | Independent integration model, legal train configurations and original speed/force/station/boarding comparisons. |
| Excitement/intensity/nausea ratings and upkeep | Research identifies inputs and source-only hypotheses; no accepted formula or implemented rating pass. | Original measurement corpus and separately labelled reconstructed/observed expected values. |
| Guest motives, queues, route choice and staff work | Individual arrivals, traits, needs, flat-path navigation, FIFO queues, price rechecks and payment reconciliation are implemented as an explicit candidate. Need-based food/drink/restroom purchases, patrol-aware mechanic repair/inspection are candidates. Bench/rest, bins/wrappers/litter and timed handyman work are integrated candidates. Exact original placement, motive/route choice, service cadence and AI qualification remain outstanding. | Cross-system production fixtures and original guest/staff comparisons; modern improved AI is not the vanilla oracle. |
| Loan, time, recurring costs, demand and profit formulas | Loan accounting, categorized sales/stock, weekly wage/interest and fortnightly upkeep candidates implemented; the reconstructed nominal clock/finance map is not exact vanilla validation. | Original calendar/payment/value/arrival observation boundaries and independent rule implementations. |
| Capacity and hardware | Reference-oriented backing map, ride slots and construction-record threshold implemented; synthetic performance evidence is bounded. | Production-rule scale/boundary/soak cases and the mandatory representative real integrated-GPU gate. |

Primary context remains in [reference overview](research/rct2-reference.md),
[track dependencies](research/track-simulation-model.md),
[guest/staff dependencies](research/guest-staff-model.md),
[economy/time](research/park-economy-model.md),
[scale](research/original-rct2-scale.md) and
[save/replay evidence](research/save-reference-validation.md).
No original executable was observed during the initial research. No commercial
game data or GPL simulation implementation is bundled. This register is not an
approval to reduce fidelity; entries close only on their actual evidence.
