## Coordinates and dependency structures

A tile is 32 XY coordinate units; stored height steps are 8 Z units and land steps are 16. Cardinal directions 0–3 mean X−, Y+, X+, Y−. Track descriptors additionally encode diagonal endpoints, relative end offsets, and starting/ending heights. [Units](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/world/MapLimits.h#L14-L33); [directions](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/world/Location.hpp#L457-L463); [endpoints](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/ted/TrackElementDescriptor.h#L112-L129).

Connectivity searches matching ride, sequence-zero, ghost status, direction and endpoint height; circuit validation additionally compares adjoining pitch/roll and detects gaps. Geometric proximity alone therefore does not establish connectivity. [Traversal](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/TrackIteration.cpp#L192-L301); [circuit validation](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/TrackIteration.cpp#L466-L495); [shape matching](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Track.cpp#L34-L48).

Placement rotates all clearance sequences and quarter-tile occupancy. Ordinary origins require Z modulo 16 = 0, or 8 for flagged half-height pieces; clearance includes vehicle height. Bounds, ownership, overlap, terrain/water and support limits constrain acceptance. Track-design placement can ignore same-ride intersections, a distinct comparison condition. [Placement](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/actions/track/TrackPlaceAction.cpp#L184-L290); [water/support checks](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/actions/track/TrackPlaceAction.cpp#L294-L399).

Contiguous station placement maintains length and begin/middle/end pieces. Entrances/exits use station height and identity, then connect footpath edges and queue chains. Presence validation aggregates portal availability across stations and rejects a station with neither; subsequent path-connectivity warnings are separate. [Platforms](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Track.cpp#L124-L233); [portals](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/actions/ride/RideEntranceExitPlaceAction.cpp#L181-L234); [presence](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Ride.cpp#L2367-L2412); [reachability warnings](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Ride.cpp#L2013-L2080).

## Motion, dispatch, and testing

Movement indexes sampled `VehicleInfo` poses by track type, cardinal direction, subposition and progress. Integer velocity plus acceleration determines residual travel; progress crosses piece boundaries, resolves the next piece and updates XYZ/yaw/pitch/roll. Train updates traverse linked cars, combine pitch acceleration and mass-dependent resistance, and apply powered-car behavior. [Sample lookup](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.cpp#L166-L184); [travel budget](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.TrackMotion.cpp#L286-L305); [piece progression](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.TrackMotion.cpp#L864-L915); [train update](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.TrackMotion.cpp#L1385-L1567).

The ordinary cycle is `movingToEndOfStation → waitingForPassengers → waitingToDepart → departing → travelling → arriving → unloadingPassengers`; specialized ride states also exist. Passenger loading, minimum/maximum wait and another train arriving establish readiness; station permission and synchronization can still hold departure. Block-section station permission depends on brake state. [States](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.h#L95-L128); [station positioning](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.Station.cpp#L420-L448); [departure to travel](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.Station.cpp#L1268-L1284); [arrival transition](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.Station.cpp#L1495-L1511); [unloading transition](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.Station.cpp#L1700-L1724); [cycle restart](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.Station.cpp#L999-L1004); [loading gates](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.Station.cpp#L589-L677); [departure gates](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.Station.cpp#L748-L768); [block permission](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Station.cpp#L62-L86).

Testing/opening validate stations, portals, mode-dependent circuit requirements and train suitability before vehicle creation. `simulate` omits the entrance/exit and complete-circuit checks used by `test/open`; its vanilla equivalence is unverified. A non-ghost departing train can start measurements when neither tested nor testInProgress is set, independently of the named testing status. Completion sets `tested`; invalidation clears ratings and test flags. [Operation](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Ride.cpp#L3787-L3920); [validation](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Ride.cpp#L5658-L5758); [measurement start](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.Station.cpp#L1054-L1071); [completion](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.cpp#L913-L954); [invalidation](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Ride.cpp#L4397-L4410).

## Rating dependencies and renderer data

Measurements accumulate speed, time/length, filtered G-forces and airtime, plus turns, inversions, drops, shelter and special elements. G-forces depend on pose, velocity and descriptor factors. [Measurements](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.cpp#L439-L510); [element/drop/shelter accumulation](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.cpp#L514-L749); [forces](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.cpp#L1158-L1183).

Ride-type modifiers select measured length, maximum/average speed, duration, forces, turns, drops and shelter, plus train length and synchronization, to adjust base ratings. Surroundings include water, terrain, queue/non-queue paths, own/other track and scenery. Normal calculations require `tested`; flat rides and stalls take separate branches. A track traversal supplies proximity scores; unmet requirements can divide ratings, and excessive intensity reduces excitement. Wooden-coaster data demonstrates type-specific thresholds and coefficients. [Surrounding categories](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/RideRatings.cpp#L54-L83); [proximity traversal](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/RideRatings.cpp#L338-L447); [calculation](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/RideRatings.cpp#L877-L1055); [requirements](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/RideRatings.cpp#L2101-L2148); [intensity penalty](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/RideRatings.cpp#L1319-L1330); [type data](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/rtd/coaster/WoodenRollerCoaster.h#L65-L92).

**Renderer requirements, inferred from these structures:** piece/sequence transforms, supports/tunnels, platform/portal geometry, colours, per-car poses and occupancy/restraint/brake states. Legal placement, connectivity, integer motion, dispatch and rating inputs remain simulation comparison authorities. Meshes, camera scale and visual interpolation need separate presentation treatment; pose samples do not supply a complete 3D asset catalogue.

## Proposed comparisons and unresolved parity

Compare mismatched endpoint height/bank, overlapping clearance, unsupported height, missing portals versus disconnected paths, incomplete circuits versus shuttle modes, and full trains held by closed blocks. Hold train/settings fixed while changing banking, a drop or scenery; record states, poses, speed, forces, measured length/time and rating triples separately.

Two documented vanilla differences deserve boundary cases: fractional minimum-load targets now round upward (six seats at quarter load gives two riders), whereas the comment identifies vanilla rounding downward (predicting one); station-length safety margins now apply only to block-sectioned rides. [Load rounding](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Vehicle.Station.cpp#L643-L652); [station margin](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Ride.cpp#L4840-L4851).

Vanilla motion constants, every piece/vehicle combination, operating-mode transitions, rating thresholds and update timing remain unverified. No proposed comparison was executed. These gaps require controlled original-RCT2 comparisons before fidelity claims.
