# Park Economics, Simulation Time, and Objectives

Evidence: OpenRCT2 commit `11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2`, inspected 2026-10-03 for [the economics research ticket](https://github.com/patrick-fu/coaster-tycoon-3d/issues/13). This maps implementation behavior; original RCT2 timing and numerics remain unverified. No game executable, assets, or benchmark was used.

## Clock and trigger map

The nominal logic rate is 40 updates/second. Speed changes the number of updates; ordinary pause suppresses simulation updates. Calendar ticks increase by four per update: 16,384 updates/month, four 4,096-update weeks/month, and eight months/year, March–October. Days are proportional subdivisions of each month's length, rather than seven-day accounting weeks. These are logical cadences, not measured browser timings. [clock](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/Game.h#L20-L27), [speed/pause](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/GameState.cpp#L100-L166), [calendar](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/Date.cpp#L18-L25), [boundaries](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/Date.cpp#L59-L120), [months](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/Date.h#L17-L28).

## Money and demand invariants

Money uses integer tenths. Successful paid actions debit quoted costs; positive action costs require available cash. Revenue enters through negative payments, which increase cash and categorized ledger totals. Gate payment occurs at admission; shop purchases separately book stock expense and sales. Thus revenue, stock margin, cash and estimated profit are distinct. [representation](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/core/Money.hpp#L14-L17), [actions](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/actions/GameActionRunner.cpp#L354-L363), [ledger](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/management/Finance.cpp#L60-L99), [admission](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/entity/Peep.cpp#L1798-L1832), [shops](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/entity/Guest.cpp#L1646-L1663).

Free-entry unlocks ride pricing and locks the gate fee; paid-entry reverses those permissions. `unlockAllPrices` permits both. Shop pricing survives the ride-price lock. No-money makes effective gate/ride prices zero, bypasses action affordability/payment and recurring deductions, while simulation and research continue. Objective validity rejects money-dependent goals without money and monthly ride income without chargeable rides. [permissions](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/world/Park.cpp#L730-L768), [ride/shop prices](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Ride.cpp#L5105-L5117), [validity](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/scenario/ScenarioObjective.h#L73-L84).

Loans transfer equal amounts between cash and principal, outside the expenditure ledger. Borrowing is capped; repayment requires cash. Company value equals park value + cash − loan, so borrowing alone preserves it after refresh. Wages and research charge quarter-month amounts weekly; interest has separate RCT1/ordinary formulas; upkeep charges non-closed rides even without sales. Daily profit is an estimate with its own loan formula, not cash reconciliation. Ride hourly income/profit uses customer-history projections refreshed every 960 updates. [loans](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/actions/park/ParkSetLoanAction.cpp#L40-L67), [company](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/world/Park.cpp#L507-L513), [costs](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/management/Finance.cpp#L109-L194), [estimate](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/management/Finance.cpp#L257-L302), [ride reporting](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Ride.cpp#L817-L839).

Ride value combines ratings, age and duplicate-type penalties; upkeep depends on geometry, vehicles, stations and operating mode. Park value combines ride value, customer history, type bonuses and guests. Demand combines rating, serviceable open rides, population, awards and entrance value relative to fares. Campaigns add separate generation attempts. Guests' affordability and willingness-to-pay still gate spending; paid admission reduces ride-value comparison to one quarter. Mechanic orders, availability and patrol coverage constrain repairs/inspections; inspection completion restores reliability. [ride economics](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/RideRatings.cpp#L1105-L1246), [demand](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/world/Park.cpp#L64-L237), [park value](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/world/Park.cpp#L492-L504), [guest price](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/entity/Guest.cpp#L1961-L1980), [value comparison](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/entity/Guest.cpp#L2109-L2134), [mechanics](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/Ride.cpp#L1521-L1564), [inspection](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/entity/Staff.cpp#L2632-L2641).

## Objectives and reference situations

Deadline guest goals require rating ≥600; park-value goals compare cached value at `8 × Year` elapsed months, with configurable early completion. Ten-coaster goals require distinct open subtypes and excitement ≥6.00, or ≥7.00 plus minimum length; five-coaster goals identify designated unfinished rides. Repayment requires loan ≤0 plus park value. Monthly ride income is gross ticket revenue; food income includes merchandise and subtracts stock. Guest-and-rating succeeds at rating ≥700 plus target guests, or fails on the 29th low-rating day after the initial month. Success/failure records a terminal result and resets speed/opens objective UI; subsequent checks become undecided. Low-rating failure additionally closes the park. Declared `none`, `haveFun` and `buildTheBest` types default to undecided. [deadline](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/scenario/ScenarioObjective.cpp#L23-L63), [conditions](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/scenario/ScenarioObjective.cpp#L71-L215), [rating threshold](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/scenario/ScenarioObjective.h#L71-L71), [terminal guard/closure](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/scenario/ScenarioObjective.cpp#L222-L267), [result handling](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/scenario/Scenario.cpp#L145-L184).

Proposed comparisons, not executed: replay identical tick-indexed actions from the same saved state at normal/fast speed and across pause; borrow/repay without construction; compare pricing modes and voucher purchases; cross a month with sales and payroll; close/break a ride while excluding mechanics by patrol; recover a low-rating streak before day 29. Record tick/calendar, ledger rows, cash/loan, cached values, rating, research stage, ride flags and objective state. The cited trigger/condition map supplies expected observation points.

Original-game parity still needs direct replay: rounding, phase offsets, cached-value lag, demand probabilities, staff response and research duration. OpenRCT2 advertises changed guest/staff AI; its five-coaster check explicitly fixes original omissions. These differences remain evidence boundaries, not vanilla requirements.
