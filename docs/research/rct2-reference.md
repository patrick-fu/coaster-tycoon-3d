# RCT2 Behavioral Reference

## Evidence boundary

The original-game baseline below summarizes the 2002 *RollerCoaster Tycoon 2* PC manual from an archival scan. OpenRCT2 describes itself as a reimplementation and lists improved guest and staff AI, interface and editing additions, higher limits, multiplayer, and RCT1 mechanics restored to the game. The source snapshot consulted below is OpenRCT2 `develop` at commit `11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2`, fetched on 2026-10-03. Every code link is pinned to that SHA. Treat code-only mechanics, thresholds, fixes, and extensions as OpenRCT2 evidence pending comparison with original RCT2. No original executable was tested. [OpenRCT2 README at pinned commit](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/readme.md#L66-L68).

## Original RCT2 manual baseline

The 2002 PC manual is an introductory guide, not a full specification. It presents coaster building as selecting a ride, location and orientation, then placing direction- and slope-dependent pieces whose options vary with ride type and preceding track. Stations require adjacent entrances and exits connected to park paths. Testing applicable rides generates excitement, intensity and nausea ratings; some ride types need no test. Readouts include speed, length, duration, forces, reliability, downtime, popularity, queue length and wait. Guests have happiness, hunger, thirst, nausea, cash, preferred intensity and nausea tolerance, and can become lost, bored or disgusted. Footpaths link the park and queues lead to ride entrances. Revenue comes from gate or per-ride admission, plus shop sales; finance tools show income, expenses, loans and profit. Handymen, mechanics, guards and entertainers have distinct tasks and can be assigned patrol areas. Scenarios set objectives, deadlines, restrictions, finances, guest traits and research order; examples include guest counts, park value and coaster length. Park rating (0–1,000) reflects overall impressions of design, layout, cleanliness, value and efficiency. Kiosk maps help prevent lost guests; weather changes food and drink preferences. [RCT2 PC manual, pp. 8, 34–40 and 48](https://rct2.rctspace.com/rct2_manual.pdf); [guest and path details, pp. 14, 30–33 and 45–47](https://rct2.rctspace.com/rct2_manual.pdf); [staff and scenario details, pp. 41–44 and 58–63](https://rct2.rctspace.com/rct2_manual.pdf).

## OpenRCT2 implementation evidence

**Track construction.** At the pinned snapshot, the placement action resolves a track-element descriptor, rotates its clearance sequences by direction, and checks map bounds, ownership, valid heights, overlaps, ride-specific water requirements, and support limits. These checks describe OpenRCT2’s engine behavior; they do not establish every original UI choice or error response. [TrackPlaceAction.cpp, pinned commit](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/actions/track/TrackPlaceAction.cpp#L184-L228); [clearance and support checks](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/actions/track/TrackPlaceAction.cpp#L267-L290); [water and support rules](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/actions/track/TrackPlaceAction.cpp#L320-L357).

**Testing and ratings.** OpenRCT2 does not calculate ordinary ride ratings until the ride has been tested. It starts from ride-type base ratings and applies modifiers for length, speed, duration, G-forces, turns, drops, shelter, proximity, scenery, special elements, and unmet requirements. These are source-level comparison variables; their exact formula and weights are not asserted as original-game rules. [RideRatings.cpp, pinned commit](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/RideRatings.cpp#L877-L949); [requirements and final adjustments](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/ride/RideRatings.cpp#L997-L1057).

**Paths and queues.** In the OpenRCT2 pathfinder, a guest seeking a queued ride targets that ride’s queue; queue tiles are associated with a ride. Path heights, slopes, edge connectivity, dead ends, and junctions affect route search. This suggests concrete network failure cases to compare, while route choice and recovery behavior remain implementation-specific. [GuestPathfinding.cpp, pinned commit](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/peep/GuestPathfinding.cpp#L841-L887); [ride-queue endpoint handling](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/peep/GuestPathfinding.cpp#L1698-L1749).

**Guest choice and spending.** In this snapshot, ride evaluation checks available cash, intensity and nausea suitability, recent crashes, weather, and price. It rejects a fare above twice the ride’s calculated value; for guests who paid park admission, that value is reduced by 75% for the willingness-to-pay comparison. These exact gates are OpenRCT2 implementation evidence pending a vanilla comparison. [Guest.cpp, pinned commit](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/entity/Guest.cpp#L1961-L2134). OpenRCT2’s finance code records loan interest and ride running costs and subtracts staff wages, research, and loan costs when calculating weekly profit; accounting values and timing remain unverified against RCT2. [Finance.cpp, pinned commit](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/management/Finance.cpp#L140-L187); [weekly cost calculation](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/management/Finance.cpp#L267-L298).

**Staff and maintenance.** OpenRCT2 explicitly describes staff AI as improved, so movement, response times, and breakdown frequencies need vanilla comparison where parity matters. The original staff roles and patrol rules remain in the bounded manual summary above. [OpenRCT2 README at pinned commit](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/readme.md#L66-L68).

**Scenario objectives.** The pinned evaluator implements guest-and-rating, park-value, coaster-count/rating/length, loan repayment, and monthly food or ride-income objectives. Its “finish five coasters” check adds validation that a code comment says original RCT2 lacked; that behavior is a fix, not an original-game guarantee. [ScenarioObjective.cpp, pinned commit](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/scenario/ScenarioObjective.cpp#L23-L64); [documented objective correction](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2/blob/11513222890717e81431c83a28aafb7555f2ccd2/src/openrct2/scenario/ScenarioObjective.cpp#L161-L181).

## Candidate fidelity checks

- Append legal coaster pieces, then try an invalid height, overlap, unowned tile, or unsupported height. Compare selected-piece previews and construction results.
- Test a completed coaster before and after adding drops, speed, banking, or nearby scenery. Record testing state, excitement, intensity, nausea, raw ride stats, and park rating separately.
- Connect an entrance through a short queue to the public path and connect the exit independently. Remove one tile or reverse a sloped segment, then observe queue access and ride exit behavior.
- Put guests with different intensity preferences, nausea tolerance, and pocket cash near one ride. Change its price and compare thoughts, queue entry, and purchases. Treat OpenRCT2’s 2× ride-value / paid-entry rule as a comparison hypothesis only.
- Give a mechanic a restricted patrol that excludes a ride, then cause or wait for a breakdown; compare repair response with an unrestricted mechanic and record inspection and downtime signals.
- Compare deadline objectives when the target is missed or met, then compare a coaster length or rating objective. Record the win/fail transition and any minimum rating condition.

## Gaps

Original RCT2’s complete rating arithmetic, guest decision cadence and pathfinding tie-breaks, exact finance timing, all construction legality cases, and every objective condition remain unverified. OpenRCT2 documents changes and fixes, and no original executable was tested; do not promote its exact numerics or corrections into vanilla requirements without a direct comparison.
