# Author editable discrete assets and separate content identities

Patrick rejected the second Classic art checkpoint on 2026-10-07 and expanded
the destination to the original RCT2 facility/category breadth and operating
systems. The existing procedural miniature models and one-family content profile
do not support that destination.

Keep the independent TypeScript worker/Three.js architecture from ADR 0002.
Separate a ride's family, object/vehicle variant, operating mode and constructed
instance. Declarative identity/capabilities and explicitly versioned saves precede
bulk catalogue exposure. Track/collision/motion/transactions remain authoritative
worker rules; appearance must not create fictional simulation capabilities.

Use a dedicated editable Blender→GLB pipeline for discrete vehicles, buildings,
stations, fixed rides, characters and props, with UV/material/rig/anchor and
provenance records. Continuous track/variable supports remain generated from
actual geometry with authored profiles/components. Retain editable model sources
and reproduce exports remotely. Do not squash asset bounds non-uniformly to hide
unverified reservations. Native collision, render mapping and visual/swept bounds
have distinct contracts; exact original dimensional mapping is still open.

Original media is an external observation corpus. Released models are independently
authored and texture sources have compatible per-asset permissions. This does not
require players to supply original files or change the MIT source strategy.

Author and remotely inspect a representative car/station/kiosk before bulk
production; then water/fixed ride/landscape and complete content batches. GLB
validation, actual browser poses, picking/disposal, scale measurements and Patrick's
visual acceptance qualify the route. The planning documents and acquired source
materials do not establish those results. New simulation/data conventions require
explicit save migration; old checks qualify only the profile they actually ran.
